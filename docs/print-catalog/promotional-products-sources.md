# Rawaj Master Print Catalog — Promotional Products & Corporate Gifts

Updated: 2026-09-27

This document supports product-first RFQ models for promotional merchandise. Rawaj should first identify the physical product and substrate, then select a compatible decoration method. A product is not assumed to be "UV", "laser" or "sublimation" merely because those technologies exist.

## Inkcups — Promotional product printing
- https://www.inkcups.com/printing-applications/
- https://www.inkcups.com/printing-applications/promotional-products/

Verified:
- Promotional products can be decorated using both pad printing and direct/digital printing depending on item, geometry, artwork and production requirements.
- Pens are explicitly documented as a common promotional-product application for pad and digital printing.
- Curved, irregular and high-volume products can require a different process from flat digital printing.
- Rawaj therefore captures product material, geometry, imprint area and artwork before confirming a print method.

## Roland DG — Direct-to-object UV
- https://www.rolanddga.com/products/printers/versaobject
- https://www.rolanddga.com/en-la/products/printers/versaobject-mo-series

Verified:
- Direct-to-object UV workflows can customize phone cases, bottles/flasks, acrylic gift items, stationery, pens/pencils and other compatible objects.
- Curved/cylindrical items may use distance/rotary workflows depending on equipment and geometry.
- UV suitability and adhesion are substrate-dependent; customer-facing forms should not promise UV on every material without assessment.

## Trotec — Laser personalization
- https://www.troteclaser.com/en/laser-applications/personalization

Verified:
- Laser personalization is used for ballpoint pens, drinkware, gifts, promotional products and nameplates.
- Material families include wood, acrylic, plastics, glass, metal and leather, but laser source/process must match the actual material.
- Laser engraving/marking therefore appears as a compatible-method option, not as a universal decoration method.

## Mimaki — Sublimation on promotional polyester items
- https://mimaki.com/application/other/entry-90607.html

Verified:
- Dye-sublimation transfer is demonstrated on polyester neck straps/lanyard-type products and other polyester promotional items.
- Sublimation is tied to polyester or sublimation-compatible surfaces; it must not be offered as a generic process for arbitrary lanyard materials.

## Epson / existing Rawaj sublimation evidence
- https://epson.com/sublimation-printers-for-makers
- See also docs/print-catalog/textile-specialty-sources.md

Verified:
- Sublimation transfers into compatible polyester/sublimation blanks and is documented for personalized drinkware/tumblers.
- A generic ceramic/metal item is not automatically sublimation-ready; the blank/coating must be compatible.

## Rawaj modeling rules

1. Product family comes first; decoration technology comes second.
2. Pens can be quoted by material, imprint area, artwork and quantity, then assigned pad, UV or laser when compatible.
3. Drinkware must capture material, shape/capacity and branding scope before selecting sublimation, laser, UV or other methods.
4. Polyester lanyards can support full-colour sublimation; other constructions remain supplier-dependent.
5. Keychains and promotional notebooks use material-specific decoration; no single method applies to all variants.
6. Corporate gift sets aggregate existing/sourced products and packaging rather than duplicating each component's technical rules.
7. Customer-supplied goods require compatibility/testing before production.
8. Electronic gifts such as power banks and USB devices are not yet activated in this batch; supplier safety/compliance and product sourcing need separate verification.
9. Pricing remains RFQ-only.
