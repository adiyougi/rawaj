## Pressure-sensitive labels

### Avery — label material comparisons
- https://www.avery.com/custom-printing/labels/calculator/rolls
- https://www.avery.com/custom-printing/labels/material/film-labels
- https://www.avery.com/custom-printing/labels/calculator/material-film-clear
- https://www.avery.com/industrial/articles-and-resources/additional-resources/materials/

Verified:
- Paper and film facestocks are different product families.
- Common roll facestocks include matte/gloss white paper, textured and metallic paper, white/clear/chrome BOPP, squeezable MDO and specialty films.
- Film labels can be specified for moisture resistance and squeezable containers.
- Matte/gloss varnish and matte/gloss laminate are separate finish families.
- Transparent/metallic labels may use white underprint.
- Roll configuration can include paper or PET liner and unwind direction.

### Avery Dennison — adhesive selection
- https://label.averydennison.com/content/dam/2026-ap-lpm-assets/amea/po/asean/po/pcg/ASEAN%20PCG%202026.pdf
- https://label.averydennison.com/content/dam/2025-ap-lpm-website-assets/english/po/asean-/po-adhesive-asean.pdf

Verified:
- Adhesive selection depends on application surface, surface energy, moisture/contamination, application temperature and service temperature.
- Permanent/removable behavior is an explicit technical requirement.
- Glass, PET, HDPE/LDPE, PP, metal, cardboard and corrugated surfaces have different adhesion characteristics.
- Cold/chilled/freezer and wet-surface applications require purpose-matched adhesives.
- Therefore Rawaj's label RFQ asks for the application surface and environment before the adhesive is finalized.

### 4over — roll labels
- https://4over.com/rectangle-roll-labels

Verified:
- Semi-gloss, clear BOPP, white BOPP and metallic stocks are commercial roll-label materials.
- White-mask printing is used on supported materials.
- Gloss/silk lamination and multiple unwind directions are commercial production options.

## Folding cartons

### Stora Enso — graphical/folding carton boards
- https://www.storaenso.com/en/products/paperboard-materials/graphical-board
- https://www.storaenso.com/en/products/paperboard-materials/folding-cartons/carton-boards-for-luxury-packaging

Verified:
- SBS/SBB and FBB are distinct paperboard families used for graphical and folding-carton applications.
- Board selection must preserve cutting, creasing and foldability.
- Premium SBS boards can support embossing, foil stamping and demanding finishing.

### Iggesund — printing and converting performance
- https://int.iggesund.com/globalassets/iggesund/services/knowledge/iam/reference-manual/rm-pdf----full-sections---en/reference-manual-printing-and-converting-performance.pdf

Verified:
- SBB, FBB and WLC behave differently in cutting and creasing.
- Grain direction, moisture, thickness and surface treatment influence die-cutting, creasing and embossing.
- Carton structure and board must therefore be quoted together, not independently.

### PakFactory — folding carton structures/options
- https://pakfactory.com/folding-carton-paperboard-boxes.html
- https://pakfactory.com/tuck-end-auto-bottom.html
- https://pakfactory.com/tuck-end-snap-lock-bottom.html

Verified:
- Straight/reverse tuck, auto-bottom, snap-lock, sleeves and other structures are real commercial folding-carton styles.
- Offset, digital and UV are available printing paths depending on supplier/product.
- Varnish, aqueous/UV coatings, Spot UV, lamination, soft touch, foil, emboss/deboss and window patching are distinct finishing/converting options.
- Inserts are a separate packaging component.

## Corrugated packaging

### FEFCO — international corrugated design system
- https://www.fefco.org/technical-information/fefco-code
- https://www.fefco.org/node/1588
- https://www.fefco.org/node/656

Verified:
- FEFCO codes are internationally used to describe corrugated packaging designs.
- RSC / FEFCO 0201 is a common slotted case.
- Slotted and die-cut boxes are different construction/conversion families.
- Corrugated conversion can include printing, slotting, die-cutting, folding and gluing.
- Flexographic printing is common; digital direct printing is also used.
- Common flute families include A, B, C, E and microflutes F/G/N.
- Corrugated board can be constructed as single, double or triple wall depending on performance requirements.

## Modeling rules established from this batch

1. A label quote must capture the application surface and environment before adhesive selection.
2. Facestock, adhesive, liner and surface finish are separate components.
3. Roll-label unwind direction belongs to converting/application setup, not artwork.
4. Folding carton board and structure are coupled by creasing/folding requirements.
5. Corrugated flute/wall construction is a performance decision; Rawaj should collect product weight/use rather than let customers guess strength alone.
6. FEFCO codes should be accepted when the customer or supplier already knows them.
7. All products remain quote-only; no automated prices are stored in the template layer.
