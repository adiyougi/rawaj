# Exhibition & portable display research

Reviewed: 2026-09-27

## Primary manufacturer references

### Orbus — retractable banner stands
- Mosquito 850: https://www.orbus.com/mosquito-850-retractable-banner-stand
- Barracuda 850: https://www.orbus.com/barracuda-850-retractable-banner-stand
- Orient 800 double-sided: https://www.orbus.com/orient-800-double-sided-retractable-banner-stand
- Imagine 800 cassette system: https://www.orbus.com/imagine-800-retractable-banner-stand

Verified product distinctions used by Rawaj:
- retractable stands can be fixed-height or variable-height;
- single- and double-sided mechanisms are different hardware configurations;
- graphic width/height is hardware-specific and must not be inferred from a generic stand size;
- carry cases, accessory channels, lighting and interchangeable cassettes are hardware-dependent options.

### Orbus — SEG / tension fabric
- Vector Frame Rectangle 02: https://www.orbus.com/vector-frame-rectangle-02-fabric-banner-display
- Vector Frame Rectangle 07: https://www.orbus.com/vector-frame-rectangle-07-fabric-banner-display
- Embrace push-fit display: https://www.orbus.com/embrace-5ft-full-height-push-fit-tension-fabric-display

Verified distinctions:
- SEG is a silicone-edge / push-fit fabric graphic retained in an extrusion channel;
- extrusion frames can support single- or double-sided graphics depending on the system;
- dye-sublimated fabric is a documented graphic option;
- optional lighting is system-specific and must not be promised generically;
- collapsible tension-fabric systems and extrusion-based SEG frames are separate hardware families.

### Orbus — exhibit kits
- 10ft trade-show kits: https://s3cdn.orbus.com/media/static/teh_event_packages_high.pdf

Verified distinctions:
- exhibit kits can combine backwalls, retractable stands, literature racks, counters/cases and lighting;
- a booth request therefore needs footprint, components, graphic faces, transport/case and lighting requirements rather than a single generic “stand size”.

The bulletin documents inkjet/screen-print compatibility and use for signs, trade-show and point-of-purchase displays. Rawaj does not infer that this film is correct for every display; media selection remains hardware- and application-specific.

## Catalog rules
1. Never treat Roll-up, pop-up, tension fabric, SEG and modular exhibit hardware as interchangeable.
2. Ask for existing hardware model/template when the customer needs replacement graphics.
3. For new hardware, capture finished footprint, visible graphic size/face count, portability, lighting and case requirements.
4. Do not promise exact dimensions until the selected hardware system/template is confirmed.
5. All services remain RFQ-only; hardware availability is supplier-dependent.


## 2026-09-27 verification addendum

- Orbus, SEG Graphics: silicone beading is sewn to the graphic and pushed into a recessed aluminum-frame channel; SEG is used across wall-mounted, suspended and freestanding systems. Source: https://www.orbus.com/seg-graphics
- Orbus, Blaze SEG Light Boxes: illuminated SEG combines frame, LED lighting and fabric graphic; mounting can be wall, freestanding or hanging, so illumination/mounting must be explicit RFQ fields. Source: https://www.orbus.com/products-by-category/blaze-seg-light-boxes
- Orbus, Vector Frame light box: commercial systems can be single- or double-sided and use edge lighting plus push-fit fabric graphics. Source: https://www.orbus.com/vector-frame-master-light-box-0404
- Displayit, Trade Show Table Covers: throw, fitted and stretch/flex are distinct constructions; full-color dye sublimation is used on polyester fabrics and the table size/style must be captured before production. Source: https://displayit.com/trade-show-accessories/table-covers/

### Catalog rules confirmed

1. Do not model SEG, pillowcase tension fabric and retractable banner hardware as interchangeable systems.
2. Replacement graphics require the hardware model/template or exact finished graphic dimensions and finishing method.
3. Lightbox RFQs must explicitly capture illuminated/non-illuminated, mounting, single/double-sided requirement and electrical/site constraints.
4. Table-cover RFQs must capture table dimensions and construction (throw/fitted/stretch); do not infer fit from nominal event-booth size.
5. These services remain quote-only; hardware compatibility is reviewed before production.
