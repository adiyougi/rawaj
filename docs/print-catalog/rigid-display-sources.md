## PVC foam board

### 3A Composites — SINTRA
- https://3acompositesusa.com/products/sintra/

Verified:
- Expanded closed-cell PVC board is a graphic-display substrate designed for high-quality printing and fabrication.
- Applications include POP/POS, exhibits/kiosks, interior and exterior signage, window displays, dimensional displays and retail fixtures.
- Recommended fabrication includes digital/screen printing, knife cutting on thin gauges, saw cutting, routing, die cutting/punching on appropriate gauges and forming.
- Published SINTRA gauges include 1, 2, 3, 6, 10 and 12.7 mm; availability depends on supplier/colour/sheet size.
- 3A specifically recommends thicker gauges for exterior applications, reinforcing that thickness/site are technical decisions rather than decorative options.

## Modeling rules established from this batch

1. Rigid substrate is a service decision, not just a hidden printer setting.
2. PVC foam, ACM, corrugated polypropylene and foam board have different fabrication and environmental uses.
3. Direct rigid UV printing is offered as a broad custom service, while common substrates retain discoverable dedicated service pages.
4. Display hardware and replacement graphics are separated: customers may buy a complete system or only a replacement graphic.
5. SEG, pillowcase tension fabric, retractable vinyl/fabric and pop-up panel graphics are distinct construction systems.
6. Exhibit hardware, graphics, accessories and installation are captured separately in the RFQ.
7. All services remain quote-only and unpriced.
