# Rawaj Master Catalog — Hardcover / Case-Bound Books

Updated: 2026-09-27

Status: technically researched. Keep commercial publication disabled until Rawaj confirms a fulfillment route.

## PrintNinja — Hardcover book printing
Source: https://printninja.com/printing-products/hardcover-book-printing/

Verified:
- Case binding is a distinct hardcover construction.
- Commercial cover systems include printed case wrap, removable dust jacket, cloth and synthetic-leather wrapped boards.
- Sewn signatures can be used before casing-in for durability and wider opening.
- Interior stock selection depends on content: coated stocks suit image-heavy work while uncoated stocks suit text-heavy work.
- Cover finishing can include lamination, foil stamping, emboss/deboss and spot UV where compatible.
- Head/tail bands and ribbon bookmarks are legitimate case-bound accessories.

## PrintNinja — cloth / PU leather cases
Source: https://printninja.com/cloth-pu-leather-books/

Verified:
- Cloth and PU cases are made by wrapping the cover material around rigid board.
- Offset printing is not directly applied to cloth/PU in this workflow; foil stamping is a documented alternative.

## PrintNinja — endsheets
Source: https://printninja.com/endsheet-setup-guide/

Verified:
- Endsheets/endleaves are structural components used to join/reinforce the book block and case.
- Their exact requirement depends on the case-bound construction and supplier workflow, so Rawaj must not promise a universal page-count rule.

## PrintingCenterUSA — binding overview
Source: https://www.printingcenterusa.com/help-center/knowledge-base/binding-overview/

Verified:
- Saddle stitch, perfect bound, spiral and Wire-O are separate binding families and should remain separate RFQ services.

## Rawaj modeling rules
1. Hardcover / case-bound is a separate RFQ service from softcover perfect binding.
2. Capture trim size, page count, interior stock, cover system, binding construction, cover finish and accessories separately.
3. Spine width is calculated from the actual page count, stock/caliper and supplier specification; never guess it in the storefront.
4. Dust jackets, cloth/PU cases, foil, emboss/deboss, head/tail bands and ribbon bookmarks are options only when supported by the selected supplier.
5. No pricing is stored. The service remains quote-only and unpublished until commercial approval.
