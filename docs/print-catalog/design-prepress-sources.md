# Rawaj Master Catalog — Design, Brand Systems & Prepress Sources

Updated: 2026-09-27

This document supports Rawaj's design/prepress service models. Creative direction itself is not reduced to technical checkboxes; the sources below define the handoff, brand-system and production requirements that affect a professional service scope.

## Brand identity and guidelines

### Adobe Learn — Brand identity workflows
- https://www.adobe.com/learn/illustrator/web/collateral-design
- https://www.adobe.com/learn/illustrator/web/design-typographic-logo

Verified:
- A brand guide documents rules that help keep identity usage consistent.
- Common guideline elements include logo variations/use, typography, color palettes, image-use/style rules, illustrations and icons; voice/messaging may also be included.
- Logo work can require deliberate variations/lockups rather than one bitmap-only output.
- Rawaj therefore separates Logo Design, Visual Identity and Brand Guidelines rather than selling them as one undefined service.

## Print-ready artwork and prepress

### Adobe Illustrator — Printer marks and bleeds
- https://helpx.adobe.com/illustrator/using/printers-marks-bleeds.html

Verified:
- Trim marks define where output is cut.
- Registration marks align separations.
- Color bars represent CMYK/process information used in print workflows.
- Bleed and printer marks are production concepts distinct from the visible design.

### Adobe InDesign — Print-ready PDF
- https://helpx.adobe.com/indesign/desktop/print/print-production-and-file-creation/produce-print-ready-pdf-files.html

Verified:
- Before commercial-print PDF export, links/fonts/bleeds/margins and print-appropriate colors should be reviewed.
- Preflight can flag missing fonts/links, low-resolution images and overset text.
- The print provider's output profile, bleed and PDF preset requirements matter.
- Press Quality and PDF/X workflows are options; supplier/job requirements should be followed rather than forcing one universal preset.

### Adobe Illustrator — Adobe PDF and PDF/X
- https://helpx.adobe.com/illustrator/using/pdf-options.html
- https://helpx.adobe.com/uk/illustrator/using/creating-pdf-files.html

Verified:
- PDF/X standards are ISO graphic-content exchange standards used in print publishing workflows.
- Illustrator supports PDF/X-1a, PDF/X-3 and PDF/X-4 workflows.
- PDF/X output includes requirements around output intent, fonts/color/transparency depending on the selected standard.
- Rawaj's customer form therefore asks whether a printer specification/joboptions exists instead of promising one PDF preset for every process.

### Adobe InDesign / Illustrator — Package and Preflight
- https://helpx.adobe.com/indesign/desktop/print/preflight/package-files-for-output.html
- https://helpx.adobe.com/illustrator/using/package-files.html
- https://helpx.adobe.com/indesign/desktop/print/preflight/configure-and-use-the-preflight-panel.html
- https://helpx.adobe.com/fonts/web/getting-and-using-fonts/package-font-files.html

Verified:
- Packaging can gather the source document, linked assets and eligible fonts for handoff.
- Preflight identifies missing/modified links, missing fonts, overset text and resolution problems depending on profile.
- Font-file sharing is license-dependent; font files must not be promised to a printer without checking licensing.
- A print-ready PDF can preserve typographic output without transferring the raw font file when terms permit embedding.

## Social-media design

### Adobe Express — Social graphics / safe zones
- https://www.adobe.com/express/create/post/social-media
- https://helpx.adobe.com/express/web/publish-and-share/share-to-social-media/optimize-your-design-for-social-media-using-social-safezones.html

Verified:
- Social content spans multiple formats/channels and should maintain brand consistency through logo, color and typography.
- Platform formats have different dimensions and safe zones.
- Static posts, carousels, stories/reels and ads should be scoped as distinct output formats rather than "one social design".

## Production-linked design rules

Existing Rawaj technical provenance also applies:
- Vehicle wrap production: docs/print-catalog/large-format-signage-sources.md
- Signage/facades: docs/print-catalog/large-format-signage-sources.md
- Packaging structures: docs/print-catalog/labels-packaging-sources.md and flexible-packaging-bags-sources.md
- Exhibitions: docs/print-catalog/exhibition-display-sources.md

Rawaj rules:
1. Graphic packaging artwork must ultimately use the approved production dieline from the converter/supplier.
2. Vehicle-wrap artwork is prepared for the actual vehicle/template/measurements and final installer workflow.
3. Signage/facade creative design is separated from electrical/structural engineering.
4. Prepress follows the actual printer/provider specification; there is no universal bleed/profile/PDF preset for every production method.
5. Creative services remain RFQ-only.
