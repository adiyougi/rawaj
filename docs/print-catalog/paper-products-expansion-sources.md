## Menus

### PrintPlace — Menu Printing
- https://www.printplace.com/products/menus

Verified:
- Flat, half-fold, tri-fold, Z-fold and accordion menu configurations are commercial options.
- Menu products use paper and cardstock families rather than one universal stock.
- Gloss/matte coatings and scoring are distinct finishing/converting decisions.
- Optional drilling is available for specific use cases.
- Waterproof/synthetic menus should be treated as a separate material class rather than assumed from ordinary paper menus.

## Tickets

## Rawaj modeling rules

1. Folders, menus, tickets, postcards, hang tags, door hangers, cards, calendars, notepads and notebooks are separate products, not one generic paper-printing form.
2. Structural conversion — pockets, folds, perforation, drilling, score and binding — is kept separate from surface finishing.
3. Writable/uncoated applications are not forced into glossy coated stocks.
4. Sequential numbering belongs to ticket/form production, not generic print finishing.
5. Waterproof synthetic products are modeled as a distinct substrate, not as a promise that ordinary paper becomes waterproof.
6. All products remain RFQ-only.
