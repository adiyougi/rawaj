# Rawaj Master Print Catalog — Paper Bags & Flexible Packaging

Updated: 2026-09-27

This provenance file supports the first Rawaj templates for shopping bags, industrial paper sacks, premade flexible pouches and flexible rollstock.

## Retail / shopping paper bags

### Smurfit Westrock — Carrier Bag Papers
- https://www.smurfitwestrock.com/products/paper-and-board/kraft-paper-rolls/retail-bags-and-wrapping-papers

Verified:
- Kraft papers are specifically produced for carrier, boutique and retail bags.
- Machine Finished (MF) and Machine Glazed (MG) are distinct surface-finish families for bag papers.
- MF provides a more matte print surface; MG has a smoother/glossier surface.
- The cited portfolio documents bag-paper grades and printing suitability. Exact grammage, strength and certifications remain supplier/product specific.

### Smurfit Westrock — Commercial Paper Bags
- https://www.us.smurfitwestrock.com/products/sacks-and-bags/commercial-paper-bags

Verified:
- Commercial bags can be square-bottom/open-mouth structures.
- Flat paper handles, twisted paper handles and no-handle variants are real commercial constructions.
- Printed retail bags are a separate converted product, not merely printed sheets.

Rawaj rule:
- Do not claim food-contact, FSC, recyclability or load performance for a Rawaj quote unless the actual selected supplier/product provides the relevant declaration.

### Mondi — Open Mouth Bags
- https://www.mondigroup.com/products-and-solutions/industrial-paper-bags/Open-Mouth-Bags/

Verified:
- Open-mouth bags can be closed by sewing or gluing.
- They may use multiple plies.
- Coatings/films may be introduced for performance requirements.
- Industrial sack design is coupled to filling, closing, transport and product-protection requirements.

### Mondi — Pasted Valve Bags
- https://www.mondigroup.com/products-and-solutions/industrial-paper-bags/pasted-valve-bags/

Verified:
- Valve sacks are designed for high-speed filling through a valve/spout.
- Valve closure can use several mechanisms depending on the product/system.
- Optional barrier layers and easy-opening features are supplier/system specific.

### Mondi — Sack Kraft Paper
- https://www.mondigroup.com/products-and-solutions/sack-kraft-paper/

Verified:
- Sack kraft includes brown, white and coated grades used for valve/open-mouth industrial sacks.
- Paper grade selection is an engineering/converting decision, not a generic customer-facing paper list.

Rawaj rules:
- Ask the customer for filled product, target fill weight, bag style if known and filling-line information.
- Treat barrier, food-contact and chemical compatibility as technical-review requirements.
- Never infer regulatory suitability from “kraft” or “paper sack” alone.

## Premade flexible pouches

### ProAmpac — Premade Pouches
- https://www.proampac.com/en-us/product/pro-pouch-premade-pouches/

Verified:
- Premade flexible packaging includes stand-up and other pouch formats.
- Closures/fitments can include zipper/reclose systems and pour spouts depending on the product.
- Clear, opaque and foil-containing structures exist in supplier portfolios.
- Barrier/performance structures are engineered to the product and processing requirements.

### ProAmpac — Converting
- https://www.proampac.com/en-us/converting/

Verified:
- Flat and stand-up pouch formats are distinct.
- Laser scoring / tear-off and pre-applied zipper systems are real converting features.
- Printed flexible packaging features should be modeled as structure + conversion + printing, not as “lamination” alone.

Rawaj rule:
- Do not advertise child-resistant, retort, aseptic, HPP, pharmaceutical or food-safe performance as a generic option. Such requirements must trigger technical review and supplier documentation.

## Flexible rollstock

### ProAmpac — Rollstock for Sachets, Stick Packs and Pouches
- https://www.proampac.com/en-us/product/rollstock-for-sachets-stick-packs-and-pouches/

Verified:
- Rollstock is supplied for sachet, stick-pack and pouch converting/filling workflows.
- Easy-opening and sealing requirements are part of the package/line design.
- Rollstock is an input to converting/fill-seal equipment rather than a premade pouch.

### ProAmpac — Dry Food Rollstock
- https://www.proampac.com/en-us/product/dry-food-rollstock/

Verified:
- Flexible rollstock is used on form/fill/seal packaging lines.
- Width/length, sealing, barrier and process compatibility are real production requirements.
- Material structures differ by product requirements and processing line.

Rawaj RFQ rules:
1. Capture the customer's filling machine type (VFFS/HFFS/sachet/stick-pack/flow-wrap) whenever known.
2. Capture web width and repeat length when the customer already has them.
3. Capture barrier as a performance requirement, not as an unsupported material claim.
4. Keep sealing chemistry, laminate construction, adhesive system and exact layer gauges in technical/supplier review.
5. All services remain RFQ-only.
